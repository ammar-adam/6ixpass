# Partner pipeline

> Use `pipeline.xlsx` in this folder: it has the dropdowns, colours and weekly counts already. `pipeline.csv` is the plain version.

`pipeline.csv` is the starting sheet. Open it in Google Sheets (**File → Import → Upload**, then **Replace spreadsheet**) and share it between Ammar and Nida.

## Columns
- **Place, Neighbourhood, Category** (Restaurant, Café, Spa, Studio, Hotel restaurant or spa, Experience).
- **Contact name, Role, Email or phone**: the person who can say yes (owner or GM, not the host).
- **How we know them**: friend, walked in, referral, Instagram.
- **Status**, one of: `Target` → `Contacted` → `Meeting booked` → `Met` → `LOI signed` → `Agreement signed`. Or `Not now` (with a reason in Notes).
- **Last contact, Next step, Next step date**: every row always has a next step and a date.
- **Offer idea**: what they said they'd offer, and which days are slow for them.

## How to fill it
1. Start with **one neighbourhood** (suggestion: the one where you know the most owners). Add 30 places you'd be proud to list. Be picky.
2. Mix of categories: mostly restaurants and cafés, a few spas and studios.
3. In Google Sheets, make **Status** a dropdown (**Data → Data validation → Dropdown**) and colour it.
4. Once a week: count rows by status. The goal for the first week is 5 to 10 `LOI signed`.

No real names are filled in here on purpose: Ammar and Nida add the places themselves.
