## More guests on your quiet days.

The 6 Pass is a yearly membership for Toronto. Members get two-for-one, or a free upgrade, at a short list of hand-picked places. They bring someone, and they come back.

### How a visit works
1. The member taps **Redeem** at the table or front desk, before the bill.
2. They show a six-digit code that lasts ten minutes.
3. Your staff check it matches and tap **Confirm**. No scanner, no app, no login at the host stand.

### You control the offer
- Two-for-one, or a free upgrade or add-on. Food, services and non-alcoholic drinks only.
- The days it runs: at least three a week. Leave out your busy nights.
- How many times each member can use it in a year (the default is two).
- Blackout dates, and a pause with 7 days' notice to members.

### What it costs you
Free for your first 12 months. No commission. No setup. 30 days' notice, either side.

### Fifty Founding Partners
We're choosing 50 places, one neighbourhood at a time, for the launch on Tuesday, March 16, 2027.
