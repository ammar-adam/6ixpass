# Image credits

Photos used in `/demo` (and later the home page). Self-hosted in `public/demo/` as WebP at 1200 and 640 pixels wide, lightly desaturated.

All were found through [Openverse](https://openverse.org) and checked by eye: no alcohol in frame, no identifiable people, no readable business names or logos. The places they illustrate are made up; the photos are not of those places.

**Why not Unsplash or Pexels:** both sites block automated access from this build environment (bot challenge), so photos could not be searched or credited there. Every photo below is CC0 or public domain, which allows commercial use with no conditions, except one CC BY photo, whose licence allows commercial use with credit (given below).
