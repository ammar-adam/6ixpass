# Showing The 6 Pass to an owner

Your demo lives at **the6pass.ca/partners-demo**. It runs on your phone, works with no signal once you've opened it once, and nothing you type leaves the phone.

## 1. Put it on your home screen (once)

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open Safari and go to the6pass.ca/partners-demo.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow, at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open Chrome and go to the6pass.ca/partners-demo.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Add** or **Install**.

A "The 6 Pass" icon appears on your home screen. It opens like an app, with no address bar.

Open it once while you have Wi-Fi or signal. After that it works in a restaurant with no reception.

## 2. Before the meeting: set up their place

1. Open the app. From the home-screen icon you land on the start screen. In the iPhone app from TestFlight you land on the member app instead, like a real app: **hold the logo** (top left) for a second to get to the start screen.
2. Tap the **gear** (top right).
3. Type the **name of the place**. Pick the **kind** and the **neighbourhood**.
4. Check the **offer**. It fills in by itself (for a restaurant, "Second main, on us."). Change the words if you like.
5. Tap the **days** it runs (at least three), and set the **uses per member** (2 is normal).
6. Tap **Choose a photo** and use one of theirs: a dish or their room, saved from their Instagram or website. It makes the biggest difference.
7. Under **Known for**, type their signature dish from the menu (for example "Cacio e pepe"). It shows on their page in the app. It's what makes the demo feel like theirs.
8. Tap **Save**.

No photo? That's fine. It shows their colour with the first letter of their name.

## 3. At the table

1. Tap **Start demo**. The member app opens with their place at the top, marked **New · Founding Partner**.
2. Hand the phone over. (To practise first, turn on **Tap hints** on the start screen: a small line on each screen then says what to tap next. Turn it off for real meetings.)
3. They tap their place, then **Redeem**. The code slides up on a pass.
4. Under **For staff**, they tap **Confirm**. ("No scanner, no app, no login at the host stand.")
5. They tap **See the owner view**. Their visit is at the top of **Recent visits**, marked **Just now**.
6. Show them **You control the offer**: tap **Offer**, **Days**, **Uses per member** or **Blackout dates** to change them, or flip **Pause the offer**. Tap **See what members see** to show that it changed.
7. Tap **Next step**. Let them scan the code with their camera to read the terms later, or tap **Email hello@the6pass.ca**.

**If they'd rather set it up themselves:** on the start screen, tap **Set up a different place** (or **Set up a place**). It asks one question per screen and takes about a minute. At the end, tap **See what members see**.

The three numbers under **How a month could look** (38, 27, 11) are a **sample month**. They are marked as illustrative. Say so if they ask: they are not real results.

## 4. After the meeting: start fresh

1. On the start screen (or on **Next step**), tap **New meeting**.
2. Tap **Yes, clear it**.

This clears the place, the photo and every visit, ready for the next restaurant. Tap **Keep it** if you pressed it by mistake.

## If something looks wrong

- **It shows the wrong place:** tap the gear (or **Edit** on the dashboard) and fix the details, then **Save**.
- **Redeem is greyed out:** the offer is paused. Go to **Owner** (bottom right) and switch **Pause the offer** off.
- **It says "Used for the year":** that place has used up its uses in the demo. Raise the uses on the dashboard, or tap **New meeting**.
- **The screen is blank or old:** close the app fully and open it again.

## Two phones (the owner confirms on their own phone)
The most convincing version of the demo: you hold the member's phone, the owner holds a phone that plays their staff. Both phones need signal (Wi-Fi or data).

**On the place's phone (Nida's):**
1. Set up the place as usual (the gear): name, photo, Known for, offer, days.
2. On the start screen, tap **Two phones**, then **This phone is the place**. A 4-letter room appears, like **W9LW**.

**On the member's phone (Ammar's):**
1. In the iPhone app, hold the logo for a second to open the start screen. Tap **Two phones**, then **This phone is the member**.
2. Type the room and tap **Join**. Their place appears, photo and all. The line under the title turns green: "Connected to the staff phone".
3. Tap **Open the app**.

**The visit:**
1. On the member's phone, open their place and tap **Redeem**. The pass shows the code and a QR.
2. Hand the owner the place's phone, on **Check a code** (or **Owner**, then **Check a member's code**). They tap **Scan the member's code** and point it at the QR, or type the six digits.
3. Both phones change at once: the owner's says **Confirmed**, the member's says **Confirmed by staff**. The visit is on the owner's dashboard, marked Just now.

If the signal drops, the member's phone has **No signal? Confirm on this phone instead** under the QR, so the demo never gets stuck.

To finish: **Two phones**, then **Stop two phones**, on each phone.
