# The demo, for owner conversations

The demo is a clickable version of the member app and the staff screen. It runs in the browser, saves nothing to any server, and every place in it is made up. Changes you make stay on your phone until you tap **Reset demo**.

Link: the preview address with `/demo` on the end, for example `https://the6pass-new.netlify.app/demo`.

## Put it on your iPhone home screen (once)

1. Open the link in **Safari** (not Chrome or Instagram's browser).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** (top right).
5. A "6 Pass demo" icon is now on your home screen. It opens full screen, like an app.

On Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Add**.

## A five-minute walk-through with an owner

1. **Explore.** "This is what members see." Tap a category (say **Spas**) and a neighbourhood to show the filters.
2. **A place.** Tap **Lantern House**. Point out the offer, the days strip (dark days are the days it runs), "uses left this year", and the plain rules at the bottom.
3. **Redeem.** Tap **Redeem** (or **Try it anyway (demo)** if it isn't running today). A 6-digit code appears with a 10-minute timer.
4. **Staff side.** Tap **See what your staff see**. The same code is waiting. Tap **Confirm**. "That's all your staff do."
5. **Back to member view**, open Lantern House again: "Confirmed by staff. You saved about $28."
6. **Your offer.** Tap **See what your staff see**, then **Your offer** at the bottom. Use **Showing as** to pick a place like theirs. Change the days, the uses per member, add a blackout date, try **Pause**. Then tap **See it as a member** to show it changed straight away. "You control all of this."
7. Tap **Reset demo** (top right) before the next meeting.

## Good to know

- The dollar savings and the weekly numbers on the staff screen are examples, not real data.
- The demo won't accept an offer that mentions alcohol, and needs at least 3 days a week. Those are our real rules.
