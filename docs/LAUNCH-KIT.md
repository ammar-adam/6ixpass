## The demo, in order

### 1. A place joins
Nida sets it up before the meeting (gear on her start screen), or the owner does it at the table. About a minute.
1. Nida's start screen. Only Nida sees it.
2. Their place: name, kind, neighbourhood.
3. Their offer: two for one or a free upgrade, in their own words. Alcohol is refused.
4. Their days: at least three a week. Leave out busy nights.
5. Their look: a photo from their website or Instagram looks best.
6. Check it: exactly what members will see.
7. "You're set up as a Founding Partner."
8. Ready: tap **Start demo** and hand the phone over.

### 2. What members see (the owner tries it as a guest)
1. Their place is first in the app, marked New · Founding Partner.
2. The place page: offer, days, uses left. They tap **Redeem**.
3. The code on a pass: six digits, ten minutes. Staff tap **Confirm**.
   Say: "No scanner, no app, no login at the host stand."
4. Redeemed, confirmed by staff. Then **See the owner view**.
5. My pass: the member's card and visits.

### 3. What the owner sees
1. Their visit at the top of Recent visits, **Just now**. Tonight: 1 member visit. You pay $0.
2. How a month could look: a sample month, marked illustrative. Say so if asked.
3. You control the offer: offer, days, uses, blackout dates. Changes show straight away.
4. Pause: members see "Paused by the venue" and can't redeem.
5. Next step: QR code for the terms, or email hello@the6pass.ca.
   Ask: "Would you like to be one of our fifty Founding Partners? It's a one-page letter of intent. It isn't a contract and it costs nothing."

Tips: **Tap hints** on the start screen turns on a small "tap here next" line on each screen. On for practice, off for real meetings. **New meeting** clears everything between places.

## What owners will ask
- **What does it cost?** Free for your first 12 months. No commission, no setup. Leave with 30 days' notice.
- **After 12 months?** We'll agree that together before then. Nothing changes without 30 days' notice. (Never quote a number.)
- **How many members?** Members join at launch on March 16. The waitlist is open now. (Never quote a number.)
- **Who pays for the free main?** You do, on the days you pick, up to the limit you set. That's why you leave out your busy nights.
- **Drinks?** No alcohol. Food, services and non-alcoholic drinks only.
- **Can I stop?** Pause with 7 days' notice to members. Leave with 30 days' notice.
