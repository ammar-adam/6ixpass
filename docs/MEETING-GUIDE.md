# Showing The 6 Pass to an owner

Your demo lives at **the6pass.ca/partners-demo**. It runs on your phone, works with no signal once you've opened it once, and nothing you type leaves the phone.

## 1. Put it on your home screen (once)

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open Safari and go to the6pass.ca/partners-demo.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow, at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open Chrome and go to the6pass.ca/partners-demo.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Add** or **Install**.

A "The 6 Pass" icon appears on your home screen. It opens like an app, with no address bar.

Open it once while you have Wi-Fi or signal. After that it works in a restaurant with no reception.

## 2. Before the meeting: set up their place

1. Open the app. You land on the start screen.
2. Tap the **gear** (top right).
3. Type the **name of the place**. Pick the **kind** and the **neighbourhood**.
4. Check the **offer**. It fills in by itself (for a restaurant, "Second main, on us."). Change the words if you like.
5. Tap the **days** it runs (at least three), and set the **uses per member** (2 is normal).
6. Optional: tap **Choose a photo** to use one of theirs, and pick a **colour**.
7. Tap **Save**.

No photo? That's fine. It shows their colour with the first letter of their name.

## 3. At the table

1. Tap **Start demo**. You're looking at their place as a member sees it.
2. Hand the phone over. The blue line at the top of each screen says what to tap next.
3. They tap **Redeem**. A code appears.
4. Under **For staff**, they tap **Confirm**. ("No scanner, no app, no login at the host stand.")
5. They tap **See the owner view**. Their visit is at the top of **Recent visits**, marked **Just now**.
6. Show them **You control the offer**: change the offer, the days, the uses, add a blackout date, or flip **Pause**. Tap **See what members see** to show that it changed.
7. Tap **Next step**. Let them scan the code with their camera to read the terms later, or tap **Email hello@the6pass.ca**.

**If they'd rather set it up themselves:** on the start screen, tap **Set up a different place** (or **Set up a place**). It asks one question per screen and takes about a minute. At the end, tap **See what members see**.

The four numbers on the dashboard (38, 27, 11, $0) are a **sample month**. They are marked as illustrative. Say so if they ask: they are not real results.

## 4. After the meeting: start fresh

1. On the start screen (or on **Next step**), tap **New meeting**.
2. Tap **Yes, clear it**.

This clears the place, the photo and every visit, ready for the next restaurant. Tap **Keep it** if you pressed it by mistake.

## If something looks wrong

- **It shows the wrong place:** tap the gear and fix the details, then **Save**.
- **Redeem is greyed out:** the offer is paused. Go to **Owner** (bottom right) and switch **Pause** off.
- **It says "Used for the year":** that place has used up its uses in the demo. Raise the uses on the dashboard, or tap **New meeting**.
- **The screen is blank or old:** close the app fully and open it again.
