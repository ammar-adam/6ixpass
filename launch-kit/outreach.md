# Outreach: what to say and send

For working through `pipeline.xlsx`. Copy, change the bits in [brackets], send. Every message comes from hello@the6pass.ca and is signed by Nida.

Never mention a pass price, a member count or a year-2 fee. No alcohol in any offer.

## The rhythm
- **Walk in first** where you can. Owners say yes to people they've met. Best time: 2:30 to 4:30, between lunch and dinner. Never Friday or Saturday evening.
- **Email or DM** the places you couldn't visit, the same day.
- **Follow up once** after 4 days, and once more after 10. Then mark it `Not now` with the reason and move on.
- **A good day:** 8 walk-ins, 10 emails or DMs, 2 demos. That's roughly 300 places touched in a month of weekdays.
- Log every touch in the pipeline: Status, Last contact, Next step, Next step date.

## Walking in (30 seconds)
> Hi, is the owner or manager around? I'm Nida, I'm starting The 6 Pass. It's a yearly membership for people who love going out in Toronto: two-for-one at fifty hand-picked places, on the nights the owner chooses. We're choosing our Founding Partners now, and I'd love [place] to be one. It's free for the first year. Can I show you on my phone? It takes ten minutes.

If they're busy:
> No problem. Can I leave this with you and come back? When's a quiet time, [Tuesday] around 3?

Leave a one-pager. Write their name and the time in the pipeline.

## Answers to the usual objections
- **"We don't do discounts."** Neither do we, publicly. It's members only, on your quiet nights, with a limit you set. No voucher sites, nothing on display.
- **"We're already busy."** Then pick only your slowest nights, or set one use per member a year. Or leave it for now; we'll check in before launch.
- **"How many members will you have?"** Members join at launch on March 16. The waitlist is open now. We won't promise a number we don't have.
- **"What happens after the first year?"** We'll agree that together before then. Nothing changes without 30 days' notice.
- **"Can we offer a drink?"** Not alcohol. Food, services and non-alcoholic drinks only.
- **"I need to ask my partner."** Of course. Leave the one-pager and the letter of intent, and book a time to come back.
